# Visual review checklist

- Top status bar reads as one composed instrument, not unrelated pills.
- Page title is the strongest text; metadata and labels form a clear scale.
- Hero progress panel communicates percent, stage, status, ETA, and cost within
  one glance.
- Lower content supports prompt inspection and a live image preview.
- Dark surfaces remain distinguishable without heavy borders.
- Crimson and cobalt/violet light guide attention without reducing legibility.
- Buttons have hover, active, disabled, focus, pending, and destructive states.
- Empty, offline, loading, locked, failed, and complete screens look authored.
- Lists remain smooth at 450 rows; full images are not loaded into every card.
- No clipped text or horizontal scroll at the required desktop viewports.
